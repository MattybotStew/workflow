# 03 — Write the PM brief

**Use in:** Phase 3, Kickoff (PM).
**Input:** `agent.md` and `research/`.
**Output:** a client-facing recap the PM can send.
**Rule:** same artifact as the manual workflow — a recap the client has seen.

---

Read `agent.md` and everything in `research/`. Write the client recap the PM
will send within 48 hours of kickoff.

The recap must resolve, in writing, four things:

1. What we are building — one paragraph, plain language, no jargon.
2. Who approves it — named people and what they approve.
3. What is out of scope — explicit, so it cannot drift later.
4. What happens next — the next two or three steps, with owners and rough dates.

Rules:

1. Write to the client, not about them. Short sentences. No design or technical
   vocabulary. If a term must appear, define it once.
2. Do not include anything the knowledge base does not support. If a decision is
   still open, say who will decide it and by when instead of inventing an answer.
3. Do not describe the process. Describe the outcome and the next step.
4. The tone is a colleague confirming a plan, not a proposal selling one.

Output a ready-to-send message: greeting, the four sections above, and a closing
line that asks the client to reply if anything is wrong. Then a short "internal
notes" block underneath, not for the client, listing open items and who owns
them.
