## What this project is

Ironwright builds precision CNC parts for small manufacturers. Their current site
is eight years old, not mobile-friendly, and buries their capabilities behind a
PDF. We are rebuilding it as a fast, six-page marketing site that leads with
capabilities and makes requesting a quote obvious.

## Who it's for

Buyer audience is a plant manager or procurement lead at a 20–200 person
manufacturer. They care about tolerances, lead time, and whether we can hold a
schedule. They will read it on a phone. Internal sign-off is Priya (owner) and
Hank (sales lead); Hank uses it daily to send links to prospects.
